DecAero: five layout mockups

All five use DecSAT's colours: the dark egui panels, the magma waterfall, cyan VFO marks, green lock, amber and orange warnings. Aircraft, registrations and numbers are invented placeholders. Every layout carries the same decoder figures as the DVB-S2 card: Eb/N0, offset, Viterbi (FEC) load, pre-FEC BER, CRC and unique-word counts, voice frame errors and CPU.

Closest to your screenshot: band, active calls and channel activity as collapsible cards in one scrolling column. Decoder figures sit in the call table.
